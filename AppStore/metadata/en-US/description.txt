Klondike solitaire with nothing in the way. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no tracking and no analytics: the game never sends anything anywhere.

• Draw 1 or Draw 3
• Standard or Vegas scoring, or none, with the Vegas balance carried between games if you like
• Winnable deals only: a solver checks each new deal before you play it
• Every deal has a number to share or play again
• Unlimited undo and redo, hints, and an automatic finish once every card can go up
• Statistics for each draw mode: games won, best time, fewest moves, streaks
• An untimed mode for a relaxed game
• Full VoiceOver support: every card can be moved without dragging
• Four-colour deck, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion and large text
• iPhone and iPad, portrait and landscape, Split View and Stage Manager, keyboard shortcuts
• In English and French
