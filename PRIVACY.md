# Privacy policy

*Klondike Solitaire for iPhone and iPad. [Version française](PRIVACY.fr-CA.md).*

The app collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash
reporting and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What it keeps stays on your device: your settings, the game in progress and
your statistics. Deleting the app deletes them. Sharing a deal number sends
it only where you choose to share it, through the system share sheet.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this
address before an app update that needs it.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/sperano/solitaire/issues>.
